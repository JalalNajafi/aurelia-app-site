What Aurelia is

Aurelia is an educational, non-explicit guide to emotional and physical closeness for consenting adult couples of every gender and orientation. It is not medical, psychological or sexual-health advice and does not replace a doctor, therapist or counsellor.

Age

You must be 18 or older to use Aurelia.

Using the guides safely

  • Every practice is optional and can be stopped at any moment by either partner.
  • Only do what is comfortable for both bodies. Adapt or skip anything that causes pain, dizziness or distress, and consult a health professional if you have an injury, a medical condition or are pregnant.
  • Consent is ongoing: either partner may change their mind at any time.
