Aurelia Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · App: Aurelia — Intimacy Guide (iOS and Android)

Aurelia is an educational, non-explicit guide to closeness for adult couples. It is designed to work without an account and without sending your personal data anywhere.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no cloud sync.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no data sold or shared.
  • Your favorites, lists, private notes and personalization answers stay in Aurelia’s private storage on your device.

What stays on your device

Aurelia stores the following only on your device, inside the app’s sandbox: your onboarding answers (goals and mood), Favorites, Want to Try and Tried Together lists, private notes, app-icon choice, lock setting, language choice, evening-reminder time, and audio playback positions. You can delete all of it at any time by deleting the app.

Face ID / Touch ID

If you turn on the lock, iOS checks your face or fingerprint. Aurelia never receives or stores biometric data; it only receives a yes or no from the operating system.

Notifications

The optional evening reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. Its text is neutral (“A quiet moment”). No push token or server is involved.

Encrypted backups

If you create a backup, Aurelia encrypts it on your device with a passphrase you choose (AES-GCM 256-bit, key derived with PBKDF2). The file goes only where you send it (for example Files or AirDrop). Aurelia never sees or stores your passphrase and cannot recover it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Aurelia receives only a confirmation of what you are entitled to; it never receives your payment details. Their privacy policies apply to the payment itself.

Children

Aurelia is for adults (18+). It is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children’s data.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because Aurelia does not collect personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf: everything is under your control on your device. You still have every right under the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on the App Store page, with a new effective date.

Contact

Data controller: Jalal Najafi, Lisbon, Portugal. Contact details are listed on Aurelia’s App Store and Google Play pages under “Developer”.
